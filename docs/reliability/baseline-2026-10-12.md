## Scope and remaining verification

This report records one successful baseline. Five sequential checkouts do not
establish capacity, overload recovery, resilience under failure, or compliance
with the 30-day checkout SLO. Poll-observed confirmation time includes polling
delay and is not the server end-to-end latency SLI.

Independent Stripe sandbox evidence has not been collected. The correctness
verifier therefore reports `NOT_VERIFIED`; application payment rows alone do
not prove absence of duplicate provider charges. Complete the provider audit,
import it through the collector and rerun the verifier before making that claim.
